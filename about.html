About PowerRank

Last updated September 25, 2026

PowerRank is an independent buying guide for portable power stations and power banks. It started from a simple frustration: most "best of" lists rank by brand recognition or simply recommend the most expensive model. PowerRank instead scores every model with one published formula that weighs capacity, outlets, output, and useful features against price, so you can see which models give you the most for your money.

How products are chosen and ranked

  • We list widely available models from established brands in each category and add new models as they launch.
  • Specs come from manufacturer product pages and listings, and are entered by hand.
  • Every model is scored by the same formula, which is described in full on the main page. You can adjust the inputs yourself with the filters.
  • No brand or retailer pays to be included, and no one can pay to change a ranking.

Affiliate disclosure

PowerRank is a participant in the Amazon Services LLC Associates Program. As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases.

Product links on this site that go to Amazon are affiliate links. If you click one and buy something, PowerRank may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. These commissions help cover the cost of running the site.

Commissions never influence rankings. Every product is scored by the same formula whether or not it earns us anything, and the formula is public so you can check our work.

About prices

Prices change often, so PowerRank doesn't display them. The value score uses a typical selling price that we research periodically. Your actual price may be different. Always check the current price and availability on the product page before you buy. The price shown on Amazon when you buy is the one that applies.

Accuracy

We work to keep specs accurate, but manufacturers revise products and listings change. If you spot an error, please let us know so we can correct it. Nothing on this site is professional electrical advice. Follow the manufacturer's safety instructions, and consult a licensed electrician before you connect any power station to a home's wiring.

Privacy policy

Last updated September 25, 2026

This policy explains what information is collected when you visit PowerRank, the websites and services on this domain ("we", "us"), and how it's used. The short version: we don't ask for or store any personal information, and we don't set our own tracking cookies. However, the third-party services described below, including Amazon, may collect information when you use the site or follow a link from it.

Information we collect

PowerRank has no accounts, sign-up forms, comments, or newsletters, so we don't collect names, email addresses, or other information you'd provide yourself. We don't use analytics or advertising cookies of our own.

Third-party services

To load and run the site, your browser connects to these services. Each receives standard request data such as your IP address, browser type, and the page requested:

  • Netlify hosts the site and may keep server logs for security and operations. See Netlify's privacy policy.
  • Supabase serves the product specification data shown in the rankings. See Supabase's privacy policy.
  • Google Fonts serves the typefaces used on the site. See Google's privacy policy.

Amazon and affiliate links

PowerRank is a participant in the Amazon Services LLC Associates Program, an affiliate advertising program designed to provide a means for sites to earn advertising fees by advertising and linking to Amazon.com. When you click a product link, you leave PowerRank and go to Amazon.

Third parties, including Amazon and other advertisers, may serve content and advertisements, collect information directly from visitors, and place or recognize cookies on visitors' browsers. Amazon uses cookies and similar technologies to record that you came from PowerRank so that qualifying purchases can be credited. We don't receive any personal information about you or what you buy. Amazon's handling of your data is covered by the Amazon.com Privacy Notice.

Your choices

You can block or delete cookies through your browser settings, and most browsers let you block third-party cookies entirely. Blocking cookies won't affect how PowerRank works, but it may stop affiliate purchases from being credited to us.

Children's privacy

PowerRank is intended for a general adult audience and doesn't knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.

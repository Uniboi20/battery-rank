PowerRank — Price Refresh Tool

Internal use only. Not linked from the site, excluded from indexing.
This talks to a Netlify function that writes to Supabase with elevated access, gated by a password only you know. Open each Amazon link yourself, check the current price, update the field, and save — one row or all of them. Use + Add battery for new products, and Details on any row to see and edit every field. You can also fix a product's brand, model, or ASIN (the 10-character Amazon product ID in its link, e.g. B0DG8JQNS4) — edited fields turn orange until saved. Use Delete to permanently remove a product that's no longer available. Saving a price that hasn't changed still refreshes its "checked" timestamp, so it's fine to resave everything even when nothing moved.
